Coloque aqui os arquivos baixados do Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com).

PERSONAGENS (nesta pasta) - Format: FBX Binary (.fbx), Pose: T-pose, Skin: With Skin
  knight.fbx     -> Cavaleiro
  berserker.fbx  -> Bárbaro
  mage.fbx       -> Maga
  rogue.fbx      -> Ladina
  paladin.fbx    -> Paladino

ANIMAÇÕES (na pasta anims/) - Format: FBX Binary (.fbx), Skin: Without Skin, 30 FPS
  (marque "In Place" nas de andar/correr)
  idle.fbx  walk.fbx  run.fbx  attack.fbx  attack2.fbx  cast.fbx
  hit.fbx   death.fbx  block.fbx  dodge.fbx  victory.fbx

ANIMAÇÕES POR CLASSE (anims/<classe>_<clipe>.fbx) — substituem as genéricas daquela classe:
  mage_idle, mage_walk, mage_run, mage_cast, mage_cast2
  rogue_idle, rogue_run, rogue_attack, rogue_attack2
  berserker_idle, berserker_run, berserker_attack, berserker_attack2 (giro do Redemoinho)
